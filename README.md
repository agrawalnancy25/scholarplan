# ScholarPlan 🎓

**Student Life & Career Planner Web Application**

ScholarPlan is a productivity-focused web app designed to help students
organize their academic life, personal goals, and career planning
in one simple and structured platform.

---

## 🚀 Problem Statement
Students often struggle to manage academics, personal growth,
and career planning simultaneously due to a lack of a unified system.

ScholarPlan solves this problem by offering a single dashboard
for planning, tracking, and self-improvement.

---

## 💡 Key Features
- 📅 Academic task & study planning  
- 🎯 Goal setting (short-term & long-term)  
- 🧠 Career exploration & planning  
- 🏡 Personal life balance tracking  
- 📊 Clean, minimal and professional UI  

---
